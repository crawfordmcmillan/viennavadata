How to read these numbers

This table lists every transaction with a sale price that the county recorded against an address inside the town in the last 12 months. It covers all property types, not just houses; the type column shows the county’s land-use label for each parcel. The county also marks which sales it verified as normal market sales. Other rows include sales tied to rezoning, sales between related parties, and prices the county could not verify. The median above uses only the county-verified single sales. The county lists some sales under more than one tax year; exact duplicates are removed here. Another {{ houses.no_consideration }} transfers were recorded with no sale price (deeds and similar paperwork) and are not listed.
