How to read these numbers

A crash appears in state records when it involved a death, an injury, or at least $1,500 in damage. VDOT grades each crash on the state’s severity scale: K means someone died, A a severe injury, B a minor injury, C a possible injury, and O property damage only. The records begin in 2017, and {{ crashes.current_year }} is a partial year. Crashes here are the ones VDOT attributes to the Town of Vienna; locations are as VDOT recorded them.
