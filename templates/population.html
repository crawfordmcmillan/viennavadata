Counts and estimates

Two kinds of numbers appear here. Decennial census figures are actual counts, taken every ten years. Everything else comes from the American Community Survey’s 5-year estimates, which are based on samples and carry a margin of error that can be meaningful for a town of this size. Both cover the Town of Vienna exactly (Census place 51-81072, the same boundary drawn on the elections map).
