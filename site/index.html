Is your address in the town?

Not every Vienna, VA address is in the Town of Vienna. The Vienna postal area is much larger than the town, so many homes with Vienna mailing addresses are actually in Fairfax County. They get county services, vote in county-only races, and are not covered by the council votes on this site. Type a street address to check.
